How QUEST works

Three simple steps from "I do not know what to play" to your next game.

1

Describe it

Type what you feel like playing in plain English. Mention a mood, a genre, or a game you already love.

2

QUEST understands

Google Gemini reads your sentence and turns it into filters like genre and tags, so you never have to pick them yourself.

3

Get real games

We look up matching games in the RAWG database, show them with a reason, and you save the best ones to your library.
