ABOUT THE EVENT

Explore the future of technology.

Tech Summit 2026 is an international technology conference bringing together innovators, researchers, entrepreneurs, developers and technology leaders from around the world.

The summit explores the latest developments in artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity, semiconductors, software engineering and emerging technologies.

Venue: Hyderabad International Convention Centre (HICC), Hyderabad, India

Date: 15 October 2026
